Integrity Measures Framework

The Integrity Management Program uses a four-step process to develop integrity measures in public sector institutions.

1

Analyze the Risks

Identify the risks, issues, and vulnerabilities that may affect the integrity of the institution. Consider their possible causes, likelihood, impact, existing controls, and remaining risks.

2

Identify the Decision Criteria

Determine the criteria that will guide the selection of integrity measures. Consider institutional values, principles of conduct, standards, agency priorities, available resources, urgency, and implementation requirements.

3

Come Up with More Than One Measure

Develop alternative integrity measures that can address the identified risks. Compare the advantages, disadvantages, feasibility, and effectiveness of the possible measures.

4

Adopt the Best Measures

Select the most appropriate integrity measures based on the identified risks and decision criteria. The selected measures should support the institution's mandate and organizational goals.
